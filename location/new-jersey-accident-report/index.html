What Is a Police Accident Report in New Jersey?

A police accident report in New Jersey is an official record created after a crash is reported to the authorities. It serves as a basic reference for insurance companies, legal representatives, and anyone reviewing what happened.

01

Who prepares the report

Accident reports in New Jersey are recorded by the responding department, which could be a local police unit, county sheriff office, or the New Jersey State Police.

02

Why people request it

Many New Jersey drivers request their report to support insurance reviews, consultations with attorneys, or for personal documentation.

03

Where the report is stored

Reports are held by the specific agency that handled the incident, which means the location of the crash determines where the record is filed.

Which New Jersey agencies handle police accident reports?

Accident reports in New Jersey may be filed by a local police department, a county law enforcement office, or the New Jersey State Police. The agency depends on the exact location of the crash, highways are usually handled by the State Police, while local streets are managed by town or city police.

How long does it take to get a New Jersey accident report?

Processing times vary by agency. Some reports are available within a few days, while others may take longer if the department is still reviewing the case. We keep you updated at every stage and share the estimated timeline once we contact the correct agency.

What information do I need to request a New Jersey accident report?

You only need basic details like the accident date, general location, and your contact information. If the agency requires anything additional, such as a specific intersection, case number, or spelling correction, we’ll notify you to avoid delays.

Can I get an accident report if I wasn’t the driver?

Yes. In many New Jersey cases, passengers, vehicle owners, or family members can request the report depending on agency rules. As long as you’re connected to the incident, we can assist with the lookup and request process.

Do you deliver New Jersey accident reports digitally?
